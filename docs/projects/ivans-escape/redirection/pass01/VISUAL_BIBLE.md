# Proposed illustrated visual bible

**Review proposal, not approved replacement art.** Keep Ivan's story and clothing identity; replace photographic cutout styling with deliberately drawn character/action paintings. Current render remains unchanged.

## Design thesis

Build each shot as an illustration first: a readable light mass, shadow mass, gaze/action vector and purposeful overlaps. The camera reveals that design; it cannot manufacture missing performance, perspective or background.

Use selective contour weight, angular face/fabric planes, two or three coherent shadow values and textured painted settings. Keep human proportions around seven heads, believable hands/shoes, restrained eyes and recognizable short hair. No copied reference characters, costume details, weapons or exact compositions.

## Palette and light

Retain #0B1018 shadow, #172330 blue-black, #586A79 concrete, #B5C3C9 haze, #E6E9E6 pale light and #C76A4B jacket. #FF4D57 is a motivated anomaly accent. Outside uses #D5CEC1 ground with pale warm sky, not an orange battlefield.

Simulation key: cool upper-left, about 35° elevation/45° camera-left, negative fill opposite. A cold back-left slot outlines the opening hero; orange remains on a limited lit jacket plane. After mismatch, introduce narrow crimson bounce from the fault side. Escape increases directional contrast, not independent lighting on every layer. Outside softens to warm back/side light.

## Character consistency

Approve MODEL before pose production. Front/side/rear must agree on face landmarks, hair mass, shoulders, jacket hem/cuffs/pockets, trouser fit and sneaker wear. Existing source/ch01-walk-a.png is an identity/proportion reference, not the new finish. Never generate independent duplicate identities.

Standing/rear/hero:2400×3600 RGBA, foot register(1200,3360). Walk/run: pelvis(1200,1900), per-cel foot contact metadata; do not normalize airborne feet to a planted baseline. Leap root: pelvis(1200,1800). Eye/hand inserts have explicit anchors in ASSET_REQUIREMENTS.md.

Performance set: four walk cels; compression/drive/extended/reaching run cels; three realization close-ups; two look-back poses; two reaching-hand poses; three launch/airborne/arrival silhouettes; one planted final hero. Reflections are physically matched views. Duplicates use exact files.

## Secondary motion

Separate one short forelock, front jacket panel and hem. Fill hidden scalp/neck/torso before cutting. Use a small project-only React group around existing Artwork/CharacterCel, not a new rig. Hold most of the time.

Routine settle≤1.2°/2 px. Awakening/action: short hair≤3°, hem4–6 px, 3–5f lag followed by explicit damping keys. Large garment changes belong in the drawings. No autonomous sine/wind/physics, elastic body bending or face morphing. Parts inherit the same pose/root; later Three shots may hold a complete character cel instead of articulating every piece.

## Shot and transition language

40 proposed shots / 39 internal cuts. Vary wide, medium, close-up and insert. C01–C13 need only flat/multiplane 2D. Reserve true ThreeCanvas for later perspective traversal, road regions and depth rows.

Hard cuts and meaningful matches dominate. Wipes come from actual painted scenery with reconstructed background and sufficient extension. Direction follows gaze, hands, feet, structure or light; no random spins or full-screen glitch presets.

Crop details from approved native-resolution art only. Enlarging a small legacy city into pavement detail is not an illustrated insert; request dedicated detail art when needed.

## Review bar

Approve the model and first-six-second compositions/light before full asset generation. The same Ivan must read across eye/hero/shoe, values must survive thumbnail size, alpha registration must be clean and foreground depth must be convincing. Existing working art is not documented as approved for this new direction.
