## Rating contract (set before pass 02 implementation)

Subjective director review, not an objective measurement or claim of reference equivalence. Each criterion is 0–10: artwork/light 30%, compositing/anatomy 25%, editing/transitions 20%, captions/readability 15%, narrative/pacing 10%. Weighted total must be strictly above 7. Any obvious broken anatomy, exposed crop seam, unintended blank frame or unreadable caption prevents a pass regardless of average. Render checks do not establish artistic quality.

Baseline concerns: photographic background mismatch, repeated face staging, floating cuff, cluttered copies, road regions with incoherent perspective, low-contrast paragraph captions and too few motivated transitions. Baseline is rejected; it is not rescored as passing by changing this rubric.

No repeated high-resolution full-film export. Use preview sections, selected frames and small review outputs. Leave Studio open.
