# FIND YOUR FREQUENCY — realme Buds Air8

Status: storyboard approved; production authorized with the user's five preferred transition styles. Brief dated 27 October 2026; production continued 28 October 2026.

## Creative direction

A 45-second, 1920×1080, 60 fps consumer product film, without voiceover. The protagonist is the user's **Master Grey realme Buds Air8**, identified from the supplied Flipkart listing. Real product photography stays recognisable throughout. The film alternates quiet material close-ups with short, decisive graphic bursts. The promise is **“Your sound. In focus.”** The campaign line is **“Find your frequency.”** Both are creative copy, not measured performance claims.

Palette: graphite `#101214`, white `#FFFFFF`, realme-inspired yellow `#FFC915`. Product finish remains its photographed colour. Typeface: locally stored Space Grotesk for large type and IBM Plex Mono for concise technical captions; both exist in the studio's project font library. No new font or engine installation is needed.

The recurring motif is an acoustic ring: a contour around the product becomes a wave, two driver fields, a battery sweep, a droplet ripple, a connection path, then a framing halo in the closing image. The visual form changes because the story changes. There are no unrelated style demonstrations.

User-approved transition vocabulary: **Liquid Morph, Bauhaus, Comic Book, Neo-Brutalism and Kinetic Typography**. The seven handoffs use liquid / kinetic / Bauhaus / liquid / comic / brutal / kinetic, with varied 0.6–0.8-second durations. All transitions operate on the complete scene, including captions, with opaque coverage and deterministic endpoints. Particle transitions are not used.

## Claim wording and readable qualifiers

Use the manufacturer evidence and retailer variant record in [FACTS.md](FACTS.md). The small print must be visible during the applicable scene, not hidden only in credits.

- ANC: “Up to 55dB; realme Lab. Performance varies with fit and environment.”
- Battery: “Buds + case; fully charged, 50% volume, normal mode. Actual life varies.” Do not imply 58 hours from the earbuds alone or with ANC enabled.
- IP55: “Earbuds only; charging case has no dust/water resistance. Do not charge wet.” Do not show the case in water or label it waterproof.
- Connection: “Enable in realme Link. Pause current audio before switching devices.”
- Drivers: use “11mm + 6mm dual drivers”; do not claim tested sound superiority or depict unverified internal construction.

No price, discount, competitive ranking, absolute silence, AI performance demonstration, universal codec compatibility or third-party endorsement is proposed. Additional researched features can remain in the facts sheet; the film needs a clear hierarchy rather than every specification.
