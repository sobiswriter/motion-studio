# One idea. Twenty worlds.

**Approved storyboard, 26 October 2026.** 60 seconds · 1920×1080 · 60fps · Motion Studio · no voiceover. The original proposal below records the approved vision; implementation decisions and review evidence live in [the production](../../src/projects/twenty-worlds/README.md).

A warm orange signal travels through five chapters: **takes form → builds a world → changes medium → breaks the grid → leaves a mark**. Each style changes its material, motion or role. The miniature movie camera introduced in clay becomes a drawing, mesh, spatial world, playing image and printed artwork. The final punctuation returns to the opening signal.

## Direction

**Visual spine:** warm orange signal, frame/aperture motif and a miniature movie camera. The film uses negative space deliberately, then breaks it at chapter peaks. A tiny style number/name identifies each passage without becoming its main subject.

**Typography:** condensed display for impact, an editorial italic for the breathing passage, plain sans for identity, monospace inside technical styles. Exact local font files would be selected and checked in preproduction.

**Materials:** clay needs thumbprints, deformation and contact shadows; glass needs genuinely occluded moving content; holography needs spectral refraction; halftone needs visible dot screens and registration; split-flap needs changing faces and hinges. Each style must show its defining behaviour.

**Camera:** macro for surface, orbit for structure, traversal for the isometric world, a stable view for working UI, hard registered cuts for print/comic. The camera moves when it reveals something.

**Sound:** keep the supplied **The Golden Hour Rise** as the proposed primary bed. Inventory its usable phrase and ending before locking the cut. Do not use **Ivan Is Loading**. Real SFX supplies appropriate recorded contacts, Openverse fills recording gaps and earshot helps select/trim/measure; procedural sound fills missing accents. The soundtrack carries continuity while sparse effects describe material. No new full score is proposed.

## The three passages to prove first

1. **9.5–20.5s: flat poster → clay camera → exploded blueprint → spatial mesh.** The same silhouette must remain identifiable through the changes; no concealed jump to a different object. Clay, line work and mesh each need a distinct motion response.
2. **30.5–40.5s: gold cinema marquee → print dots → comic page → brutal poster.** The arch, camera crop and moving orange signal stay registered while texture, composition and timing change. This is the artwork-heavy section and needs original developed assets.
3. **47–60s: flap mechanism → neon path → kinetic type → recap → identity.** This must feel like a deliberate escalation followed by release. The logo lands by 57s so the ending has reading time.

## Proposed production order after vision review

1. Prepare a handful of original hero artboards at delivery aspect ratio: clay camera, isometric district, halftone/comic artwork and final identity. Match artwork quality before multiplying scenes.
2. Make a rough 60-second music-synced animatic with these exact slots. Test reading time and energy at normal playback speed; retain every style and the full ending.
3. Develop the three difficult passages above into short motion proofs and compare them to the selected references. Fix pivots, intermediate states, material response and camera continuity.
4. Complete the film using the installed authoring tools, then sound design and mix. Run the master's applicable four review rounds with actual recorded fixes; inspect moving playback and boundary frames, final mix and delivery format.

## What to review in this proposal

- Does the orange-signal/camera story feel like a coherent identity for Motion Studio?
- Is the mix of tactile 3D, editorial print and forceful typography the direction you want?
- Should any passage get more breathing room at another passage's expense while retaining all 20 styles?

## Practical limits

Twenty styles in sixty seconds means roughly two to three seconds per style. Each passage can prove one strong mechanism; a full product story inside every style would overcrowd the film. The interactive boards are schematic proposed keyframes, not final artwork or proof of finished lighting, simulation or transitions. Music phrase timing, detailed asset production and rendering remain for the production phase.
