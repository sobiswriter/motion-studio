# Revision direction

Chosen: **Beyond the Frame.** A bevelled studio M escapes a typographic frame, becomes twenty materials and worlds, and returns as a finished studio signature. The supplied song controls the energy; a macro opening, a fast middle and a held closing provide a beginning, development and payoff.

Other films considered: **The Motion Factory**, an isometric production line with twenty stations; **Print / Play**, a collage of moving magazine spreads. Those would narrow the 3D and material range. The user authorized autonomous creative decisions and explicitly rejected the previous specimen-like edit.
