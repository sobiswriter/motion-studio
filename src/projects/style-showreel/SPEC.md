# Motion Studio / One idea. Twenty ways to move.

60 seconds, 1920×1080, 60 fps, 3600 frames. 120 BPM, 30 frames per beat, six beats per style. No voiceover. The hero is a form whose material changes, with brief full-frame geometric reveals connecting scenes. Headlines use Space Grotesk, editorial styles EB Garamond, technical styles IBM Plex Mono. Primary type is 86–310 px; style identifiers 34 px. Copy settles within 0.74 s; action continues in the material.

Palette: one ground, one ink, one accent per scene, with the explicit style exceptions required for holography, gradient mesh and Bauhaus. Readable text stays inside 192 px horizontal margins. Materials fill at least 40% of the frame. All animation is sampled from scene time; particles and texture use deterministic index hashes.

Banned: generic feature cards, slide-deck cuts, unattended loops, unseeded randomness, external runtime assets, tiny slogans, generic gradient backgrounds outside the requested mesh/holographic styles. Glass, neon, particles, halftone and labels are intentional demonstrations of the user's requested styles.

Sound: original 120 BPM score from earshot-rendered tuned/percussion sources, with coherent procedural accents and licensed tactile recordings placed through Real SFX. Final mix target approximately -14 LUFS, ≤-1 dBTP.
