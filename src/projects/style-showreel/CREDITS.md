# Sound and asset credits

Music: original 120 BPM arrangement sequenced locally from earshot 0.4.0 generated mallet, percussion and bass sources. Procedural effects: earshot launch kit, 72 locally generated takes. These are code-made sounds, not represented as recordings. Space Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono: local OFL font files with licenses in assets/fonts. EB Garamond: Google Fonts / SIL OFL, resolved locally by HyperFrames.

Recorded effects sourced through Openverse:
- “MECH-KEYBOARD-01.wav” by newagesoup — CC0 1.0. Source: https://freesound.org/people/newagesoup/sounds/348239 ; license: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Trimmed and mixed.
